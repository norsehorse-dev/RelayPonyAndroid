RelayPony 4 is a redesign with stronger pairing.

• New Home: pick Files, Photos, Folder or Text, then choose who gets it
• Receiving is automatic while the app is open
• Pair with a QR code or a short word code, both ways at once
• Only paired devices can send to you, unless you allow others in Advanced
• Send folders. Text and links arrive with Copy and Open
• Unpair from Settings, and the other device is told
• Security hardening throughout
