Send files to your paired devices over the internet, not just on the same network.

• Send over the internet: RelayPony connects the two devices directly when it can, and falls back to passing the encrypted transfer through the relay when a direct link can't be made (common on mobile data)
• Relay server setting: use the default relay or point RelayPony at one you run yourself
• Identity backup and address book export, protected by a passphrase
• New help, licenses and about sections in Settings
• Now available in Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Hindi and Brazilian Portuguese

3.0.1 fixes the source build of 3.0, which was pinned to an older copy of the PonyDirect transport library.
